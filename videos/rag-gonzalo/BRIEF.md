## Intent

Supporting video for slide G5 ("Cuando el que sabe no está") in Gonzalo's block (Nivel 3 · Valor)
of the talk "Adoptar IA hoy" (UFRO, 28-09-2026). Gonzalo narrates live over it (see `GUION.md`,
section "G5"). Goal: the audience understands what RAG is and why it changes what a professional
can solve.

Same art direction as the deck ("G · Tablero compartido"): dotted canvas `#F3F5FA`, ink `#161A2E`,
Gonzalo's amber cursor `#FFB938`, the AI's violet cursor `#8B5CF6`.

## Data rule

Everything shown as query, fragment, vector, map, retrieved ticket, similarity, answer or workflow comes
from `data.js`, generated by `python demo-rag/rag.py export` from the real run over the vault
(`demo-rag/salida/*.json`). Only beat 1 (the expert on vacation, the incoming ticket, the calls) is an
illustrative dramatization; its SLA figures (23 % vs 81 %) come from `wiki/sintesis/metricas.md`.

## Beats (seconds)

| Beat | Time | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0–16 | The expert is away: Gonzalo's card flips to "De vacaciones", urgent ticket arrives, calls, SLA 23 % vs 81 % |
| 2 | 16–40 | 40 tickets → fragments (real sections of TK-2025-0112) → one real embedding → 304 dots in the vector DB (real t-SNE) |
| 3 | 40–64 | Natural-language query typed, lands on the map, lines to the retrieved fragments, ticket cards with similarity, answer with citations |
| 4 | 64–80 | The answer becomes a 5-step workflow, each step with its source ticket, checked off |
| 5 | 80–90 | G1 question returns: "¿Qué problema eres capaz de resolver hoy que antes no podías resolver?" |

## Audio

Music bed synthesized locally (`tools/make_audio.py`, numpy, deterministic, royalty-free) plus SFX from the
bundled media-use library, pre-mixed into `assets/audio/soundtrack.wav`. The bed sits low under Gonzalo's
live voice; SFX mark the beats.
